Frequently Asked Questions

  • Why did my scores reset after clearing browser data? Mini Play Arcade stores guest progress locally in your browser's LocalStorage rather than on a remote account server. Clearing site data resets local scores.
  • Are all games free to play? Yes—every game on Mini Play Arcade is 100% free to play in your browser.
