1. Overview & Zero-Account Design

MINI PLAY ARCADE provides free instant-play browser games without requiring visitors to register an account, sign in, or submit personal profile data to a backend database.

2. Information Stored Locally in Your Browser (LocalStorage)

To save your gameplay experience across visits, this website stores non-sensitive preferences locally on your own device using your browser's localStorage:

  • Your optional guest display handle and chosen emoji avatar.
  • Your personal best game scores, recent game history, favorite games, unlocked in-game items, daily challenge progress, and achievements.
  • Your audio mute setting and cookie/advertising consent choice.

This LocalStorage data stays inside your web browser. You can reset it at any time from the Guest Player Profile modal or by clearing site data in your browser settings.

3. Server Logs & Hosting Infrastructure

Like most static websites hosted on providers such as Vercel or Render, our hosting provider may automatically process standard technical request metadata (such as IP address, browser user-agent, referring page, and request timestamp) strictly to deliver web files securely and prevent abuse.

When third-party advertising (such as Google AdSense) is enabled on this website:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads to visitors based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Visitors may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (My Ad Center) or www.aboutads.info/choices.
