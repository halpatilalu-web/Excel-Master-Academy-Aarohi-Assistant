1. How We Use Cookies & Browser Storage

MINI PLAY ARCADE keeps data storage lightweight and transparent. We distinguish between essential local browser storage required to remember your game progress and optional third-party advertising cookies used when Google AdSense is active.

2. Categories of Storage

  • Essential Functional Storage (LocalStorage): Keys mpa_player_state_v1 and mpa_cookie_consent_v1 store your high scores, favorites, unlocked levels/skins, sound toggle, and consent choice locally in your browser. No tracking across external sites occurs via these keys.
  • Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense — When Enabled): When advertisements are activated in site configuration, Google and certified ad partners may set cookies (such as DoubleClick/AdSense cookies) to cap ad frequency, prevent invalid traffic, and—where consented—personalize advertisements. When personalized advertising consent is declined, Mini Play Arcade signals requestNonPersonalizedAds = 1. Note: Publishers serving traffic in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland must also enable a Google-certified CMP (Consent Management Platform) inside their Google AdSense Privacy & Messaging dashboard before serving live ads.
