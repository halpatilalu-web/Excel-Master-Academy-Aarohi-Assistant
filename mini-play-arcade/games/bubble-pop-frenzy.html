📖 How to Play Bubble Pop Frenzy

  1. Tap or click any connected cluster of 2 or more matching colored bubbles to pop them.
  2. Larger clusters award exponentially higher points and charge spawning of Special Bubbles.
  3. Pop consecutive clusters within 1.6 seconds to climb all the way to a 10x Frenzy Multiplier.
  4. Trigger Bomb (3x3 blast), Laser (full row), Prism (color wipe), and Chrono (+5s time) bubbles before the clock hits zero!
