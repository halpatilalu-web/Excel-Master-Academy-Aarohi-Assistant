📖 How to Play Flip Master

  1. Press and hold to crouch and build jump power toward the next platform.
  2. Release to launch into the air and collect floating energy rings along your arc.
  3. Hold again while airborne to tuck and rotate through frontflips or backflips.
  4. Release before touchdown so your jumper straightens out and sticks the bullseye!

🎮 Controls & Details

Controls: Hold Touch / Mouse / Space to charge jump, release to leap, hold in mid-air to spin, release to lock landing.

Launch between platforms of varying height and distance, spin daring backflips for multipliers, and nail clean landings.
