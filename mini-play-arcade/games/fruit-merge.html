📖 How to Play Fruit Merge

  1. Slide horizontally to position your drop laser inside the container.
  2. Release, click, or press Space to drop the current fruit—check the Next preview to plan ahead.
  3. When two identical fruits touch, they merge into the next larger fruit tier.
  4. Chain merges quickly for up to a 4x combo multiplier, and keep the stack below the top danger line!
